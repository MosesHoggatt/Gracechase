# Gracechase growth and revenue strategy

For Moses Hoggatt. Written November 4, 2026. This is the long-range plan. How the team runs it day to day is in `playbook.md`. The 8-week calendar is `post-schedule.md` and `schedule.json`.

## The goal

Build a revenue stream that keeps growing. Money follows audience, so the first job is getting more people to watch and listen. The Hoggatts make the songs and videos. The team does the rest and asks only when it has to.

Both took off between November and December 2025. The All I Need live concert video went up November 3, and its Short is scheduled for November 5.

- The Sackbuts: their own channel, two singles, one lyric video. The Christmas album is on hold until the end of this week.

What this means: the songs are fine. The problem is reach. Christmas is the one time of year our audience already comes looking for us, and it starts now.

## Principles

- **Every post points to a real song.** More volume comes from the 80-song catalog, not from filler with no song behind it.
- **YouTube is home base.** Every Short, Reel, TikTok, pin, and post sends people to a YouTube video, and the YouTube video points to the stores. YouTube is where views turn into subscribers and ad money.
- **Christmas first, through January 6.** We have two Christmas albums, a Christmas single, and two proven Christmas videos. From November 15 to December 25, most posts are Christmas songs, and Sundays are worship songs.
- **Approve in batches.** New clips, cards, and thumbnails are made in batches of 10 to 20 and approved on the board. After approval they count as approved content and can post without another ask. One batch can feed two weeks of posts.
- **Learn every week.** Each Monday review keeps what worked, drops what didn't, and records it in `learnings.md`.
- **Keep the voice.** The voice guide in `gracechase-plan.md` still applies. Worship is to the Lord Jesus Christ. Characters are friends, never couples, and Mercy and Rowan are siblings. Don't name characters unless a campaign file allows it. AI is disclosed in the credit line, without apology. Never suggest a real venue or that the family performed in person.

## Revenue levers, in order of value for the effort

1. **YouTube ad revenue.** Gracechase is in the Partner Program; confirm on the Studio Earn tab. Every upload: ads on, and once Interstreet claims the video, allowlist it in DistroKid and turn Watch Page ads on (`preferences.md`). Christmas ad rates are the highest of the year. Long videos earn. Shorts mostly drive discovery.
   - **Watch hours.** People play Christmas music in the background for hours. Two long videos are proposed for owner approval: "Christmas Window (Full Album)," made from approved art and the album audio, and a "Gracechase Christmas Worship, 1 Hour" compilation of our existing Christmas videos and audio. Either one can add more watch time than many Shorts.
   - **Partner Program thresholds.** These matter for The Sackbuts. Fan funding (Super Thanks, memberships): 500 subscribers, 3 public uploads in the last 90 days, and either 3,000 watch hours in 12 months or 3M Shorts views in 90 days. Ad revenue: 1,000 subscribers and either 4,000 watch hours in 12 months or 10M Shorts views in 90 days. Check the current numbers in Studio before relying on them. The Sackbuts get there through the collaboration credit on every upload, Gracechase Shorts, the Christmas album, and a steady release schedule.
2. **Content ID and Social Media Pack.** When other people use our songs in Shorts, Reels, or TikToks, we get paid through DistroKid (80%). Every new release gets the pack. Check in DistroKid that the TikTok and Meta music libraries are selected for each release, so creators can find our songs.
3. **Streaming royalties.** These grow with monthly listeners and saves.
   - Pitch every new release to Spotify editorial at least 7 days before release day.
   - Keep the Artist Pick current.
   - Approve Canvas loops for the Christmas songs.
   - Build two of our own playlists ("Christian Christmas Worship" and "Gracechase Essentials") that mix our songs with well-known ones, and share them.
   - Release a single every 4 to 6 weeks so followers keep getting Release Radar.
4. **Songwriter royalties we may be missing.** Free, one-time setup, owner only.
   - A PRO (ASCAP or BMI) and The MLC for composition royalties.
   - SoundExchange, for digital performance royalties as both artist and rights owner. Pandora, SiriusXM, and webcasters pay through it, and DistroKid does not collect it.
   - This is likely the biggest leak. It is on the board.
5. **Fan support.** Turn on Super Thanks and, later, channel memberships on the Gracechase channel. Phase 2: a Bandcamp page for the Christmas albums (name your price, digital gifts, Bandcamp Fridays).
6. **Sync and church licensing.** Phase 3.
   - CCLI SongSelect could pay when churches use our worship songs (Behold the King, All I Need, Your Majesty), but it needs chord charts.
   - US copyright on AI-generated audio is limited. The lyrics are ours. Read each library's AI rules before submitting.
7. **Merch.** Later. A YouTube merch shelf (Spring or Printful) with the tagline and album art, once comments ask for it. Aim for Christmas 2027.

## How we grow the audience

1. **Christmas resurfacing.** The two proven songs took off last November and December. We make sure they come back up this year with fresh Shorts, Reels, and TikToks from those videos, a Community post, and a small paid test.
2. **Cut every long video into clips.** One long video becomes 5 to 10 vertical clips (15 to 45 seconds, song title in the first second, lyric on screen), each with a custom thumbnail, approved in a batch, then posted as a Short, a Reel, a Facebook Reel, and a TikTok on different days.
3. **One song, many posts.** Each song can also produce lyric cards, a quote post, a credits or making-of post (credit line: "Written by the Hoggatt family. Brought to life with AI."), a carousel, a poll ("Which line is yours?"), and a Sunday worship post.
4. **YouTube habits.** Custom thumbnails on everything. A/B tests on promoted videos. End screens and pinned comments on every upload. Playlists ("Christmas Window," "Christian Christmas Worship," "Music Videos"). Reply to every comment in the first 24 hours. Search-friendly titles that keep the released song title ("Christmas Worship," "Christian Christmas Song").
5. **Cross-promote The Sackbuts.** Collaboration credit on every Sackbuts upload, a Featured Channels section, end screens, and Gracechase Shorts of Sackbuts songs, after the hold ends.
6. **Release often.** The family's next songs land on a calendar with a pitch, a Short, and site pages ready on release day.
7. **Website as a hub.** Every release gets a landing page with smart links. The newsletter signup stays visible. The site is updated within a day of each release, after Moses sees the preview.

## Weekly review (Mondays, by 10am MT)

A Grok agent writes `social/reviews/YYYY-MM-DD.md` and pings the manager.

- Last week's numbers per platform, from the APIs where possible and Studio, Spotify for Artists, and Ads Manager where not. List the ones still missing.
- The top 3 and bottom 3 posts, and why.
- Ad spend against the bar.
- One decision per platform: keep, change, or drop.
- Plan changes, written into `schedule.json`.
- At least one new line in `learnings.md`.

The owner sees a 5-line digest on the board. Only a real decision becomes a board task.
