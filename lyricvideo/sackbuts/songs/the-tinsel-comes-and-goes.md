# The Tinsel Comes and Goes

- **Status:** Lyrics done. Audio not started.
- **Type:** Christmas
- **Story:** Earl walks Mama's Nebo County living room from her first Christmas as a bride in 1956 to this year. The trees, lights, and gadgets all change, but the dollar manger never leaves the table, and Christ stays at the center (Hebrews 13:8; Luke 2:7 and 2:11 in the bridge).
- **Sound:** Countrypolitan (Nashville-sound) Christmas ballad in 4/4: soft strings, piano, pedal steel, and a small gospel choir on the chorus. One sound start to finish.
- **Featured member:** The whole band. Earl sings lead, and Bedgo's one spoken line before the bridge is the key to the song.
- **Video note:** Christmas Through the Decades. Verse 1 is 1956, Verse 2 is 1974, Verse 3 is 1998, and Verse 4 is now, and every chorus comes back to the same manger on the same table.

- **Verse and bridge counts.** Every verse line in all four verses is 14 syllables, so each couplet matches. "Every" is counted as two syllables (ev-ry), and "Firehose" as two. If Suno sings "Fire!" as two syllables, Verse 2 line 3 goes to 15. That's fine; leave it. Bridge lines 1 and 2 are 15 and 15. Bridge lines 3 and 4 are 14 and 14.

Mama married at about twenty in 1956, so she's ninety now.
