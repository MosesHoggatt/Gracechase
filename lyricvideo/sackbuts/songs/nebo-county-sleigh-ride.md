# Nebo County Sleigh Ride

- **Status:** Lyrics done. Audio is the wav in `E:\Music\The Sackbuts\Songs\Nebo County Sleigh Ride\`. Cover not started.
- **Type:** Christmas
- **Story:** The van will not start, so the band takes Granddaddy's sleigh and Deuteronomy ("Dewey") out with the church's Christmas baskets. Dewey only moves for a line of Scripture (John 11:35; Luke 2:14).
- **Sound:** Cowboy Christmas. Sleigh bells, a trotting clip-clop, accordion, and fiddle. The bridge drops to soft guitar and pedal steel.
- **Featured member:** The band. Dewey gets the last word.

## Lyrics

```
[Intro]
[Sleigh bells and trotting clip-clop rhythm, accordion and fiddle]

[Verse 1]
Well, the snow came down on Nebo like a feather-bed fight,
And The Furnace wouldn't start for love or money that night,
But the church had Christmas baskets for the cabins up the ridge,
So we hitched up Granddaddy's sleigh and headed for the bridge.

[Spoken Word]
Now, Granddaddy's horse was named Deuteronomy.
We call him Dewey.
He's twenty-six years old, deaf in one ear,
and he won't move one inch unless you quote him Scripture.

[Verse 2]
Well, Earl said, "Giddyup," and Dewey didn't twitch,
Luther tried to sweet-talk him and like to fell in the ditch,
Then Bedgo mumbled "Jesus wept" from underneath his hat,
And Dewey took off runnin' like a jackrabbit at that.

[Chorus]
[Cowboy harmonies]
Ride on, ride on, through the Nebo County snow,
Sleigh bells a-jinglin' everywhere that we go,
So hyah now, ol' Dewey, keep a-trottin' on by,
There's a lamp in the window and a star up high.
Hallelujah, Merry Christmas, ride on!

[Verse 3]
We left a ham at Brother Ike's and quilts down at the Coles',
Peppermint for the young'uns and some coffee for the old,
But every time we passed a church, ol' Dewey stopped to pray,
Took us forty extra minutes, there's six churches on the way.

[Spoken Word]
Earl, he stopped at the Methodist church too.
Well, Luther, I reckon Dewey's open-minded.

[Chorus]
[Cowboy harmonies]
Ride on, ride on, through the Nebo County snow,
Sleigh bells a-jinglin' everywhere that we go,
So hyah now, ol' Dewey, keep a-trottin' on by,
There's a lamp in the window and a star up high.
Hallelujah, Merry Christmas, ride on!

[Instrumental Break]
[Fiddle and accordion trade the melody over the clip-clop rhythm, sleigh bells, whip crack]
Hyah!

[Bridge]
[Slower and tender, soft guitar and pedal steel, sleigh bells hushed]
The last name on the list was Miss Hattie up the hill,
Ninety-one and widowed, and a-livin' there still,
With a candle in her window like she'd waited all year,
She said, "I asked the Lord for Christmas, and He sent y'all here."
So we sang the old carols by the light of her stove,
And she sang 'em right along with us, just like long ago.
Then we stepped out in the cold, and the whole sky was bright,
Like the Lord had hung the lanterns just to guide us home tonight.

[Spoken Word]
Ride home was quiet.
Even Dewey.
Bedgo was wide awake for once, just lookin' up at all them stars.
Said, "Reckon that's how the shepherds felt."

[Final Chorus]
[Key change, full cowboy harmonies, sleigh bells and church bells]
Ride on, ride on, through the Nebo County snow,
Sleigh bells a-jinglin' everywhere that we go,
So hyah now, ol' Dewey, keep a-trottin' on by,
There's a lamp in the window and a star up high.
Hallelujah, Merry Christmas, ride on!
Hallelujah, Merry Christmas, ride on!

[Outro]
[Clip-clop slows down, sleigh bells]
[Spoken Word]
Got home about midnight, and Dewey stopped dead at the barn door.
Earl, you gonna quote him somethin'?
"Glory to God in the highest," Dewey.
[Horse whinny, sleigh bells trot away and fade]

[End]
```
